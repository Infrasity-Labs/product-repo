Frequently asked.

Who is Upstream built for?

DevTool, AI agent and B2B SaaS startups whose buyers are engineers. Prompt sets, source tracking and listicle outreach are tuned for how developers search: SDKs, alternatives and how-tos.

Which AI engines do you track?

Eight: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot and Grok. Every tracked prompt runs on all of them.

Why track Copilot and Grok?

Copilot sits inside VS Code, GitHub, Edge and Microsoft 365, right where engineers work. Grok pulls from a different set of sites than ChatGPT does. Being cited in one does not mean being cited in the other.

How often do you check the answers?

Each scan runs your prompts across every engine and stores the full answer. Scan health shows which engines ran, when they last ran, and how complete each scan was, before you read any numbers.

How is this different from an SEO rank tracker?

AI engines do not return ten blue links. They write one answer and cite a few sources. Upstream tracks whether you are named, in what order, and which GitHub repos, Reddit threads and listicles the engine trusted to get there.

Can your team do the work for us?

Yes, optionally. The Done for you plan adds Infrasity’s engineers: content written by engineers, listicle outreach and placements, and a monthly report with a strategy call.

What’s in the free audit?

A first report in 48 hours with no install: where you are cited today across the 8 engines, who gets recommended instead, which sources engines trust in your category, and what to ship next.
